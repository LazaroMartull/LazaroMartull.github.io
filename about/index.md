## About Me

Hi, I’m **Lazaro Martull** — an AI Engineer and Data Scientist with an **M.S. in Data Science & AI from the University of Houston–Clear Lake**.

My work focuses on building **applied AI systems and intelligent software**, including large language model (LLM) applications, machine learning models, and data-driven platforms. I enjoy designing **end-to-end systems** that connect data, AI models, and user-facing applications to solve real-world problems.

I am also a **co-author of the research paper _“LeafTutor: An AI Agent for Programming Assignment Tutoring”_ (arXiv 2025)**, which explores how AI agents can provide structured programming guidance comparable to human tutors.
